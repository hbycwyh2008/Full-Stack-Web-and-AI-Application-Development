# Talk Robin Rules

**Talk Robin** = structured **pair** discussion. Assign pairs; rotate across lessons when helpful.

---

## Timing (4–5 Minutes Total)

| Step | Duration | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Student A speaks | 45 seconds | A explains to B |
| Student B speaks | 45 seconds | B explains to A |
| Pair shared answer | 30 seconds | One best answer or one question for teacher |
| Teacher cold call | 2–3 minutes | Teacher calls 2–3 students |

**Use a timer.** Practice concise answers.

---

## Two Rounds Per Lesson

### Round 1 — After Entry Check (30–34 min)

**Purpose:** Check basic understanding and **reveal confusion** before Teacher Pattern Explanation.

### Round 2 — Before Exit Evidence (72–76 min)

**Purpose:** Summarize the **core pattern** and prepare **Exit Evidence**.

---

## How to Speak (45 Seconds)

1. **Claim** — “I think a commit is…”
2. **Evidence** — “In my repo, my last commit…”
3. **Question** — “I’m still unsure about…”

Do not read entire README aloud.

---

## Pair Rules

- Both students speak every round
- Hints OK — do not give full solutions
- If AI was used, be ready to say what **you** changed

---

## Teacher Cold Call

When called, answer in **your own words**. It is OK to say what you tried before asking for help.
