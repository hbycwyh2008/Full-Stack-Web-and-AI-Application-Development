# Lesson 07 — Conduct User Research

## Mission
Collect usable evidence while separating what a participant said, what you observed, and what you inferred.

## Core Artifact
Anonymized research notes in `research/sessions/`.

## Launch Challenge — 0–10
Students label sample notes as **quote / observation / interpretation** and correct notes that mix categories.

## Evidence / Resource Scan — 10–25
Review the team's final protocol, consent boundary, and note-taking roles. No new teacher questionnaire is introduced.

## Team Sensemaking — 25–40
Teams rehearse roles and agree on what counts as a useful follow-up. Each member states one bias they need to watch for.

## Studio Build — 40–65
Conduct interviews, contextual inquiry, or structured observation as appropriate. Notes must avoid unnecessary identifying information.

## Critique / Test — 65–80
Researchers compare notes from the same session. Resolve discrepancies by returning to the evidence, not by voting on which interpretation sounds better.

## Ship Evidence — 80–90
Commit cleaned, anonymized notes. Add three **candidate insights**, each linked to evidence, but do not finalize the design yet.

## Success Evidence
Students can show where each claimed insight came from.
