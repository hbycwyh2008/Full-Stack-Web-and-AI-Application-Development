# Phase 2: AI Literacy Foundation

## Phase Goal
Build a **unified** AI literacy foundation after Git and Notion — store evidence in `ai-literacy/` on GitHub.

## Key Concepts
- AI, ML, generative AI, LLMs; limits, hallucinations, bias, privacy, responsible use.
- Direct connection to AI School Assistant.

## Required Student Output
- Study notes (AI for Everyone, Generative AI for Everyone).
- Three+ AI Lens responses; Responsible AI Checklist.
- `ai-literacy/` folder in `student-portfolio`.

---

## Suggested Lesson Count
3 lessons.

## Lesson Sequence

### Lesson 1: What Is AI?
- **Goal:** Define AI, ML, generative AI, LLMs.
- **Warm-up:** "I think AI is ___"
- **Mini Lesson:** A1 + A2 intro videos; study guides.
- **Independent Work:** Start `ai-literacy/README.md` and notes.
- **Homework:** AI for Everyone modules.

### Lesson 2: Limits and Risks
- **Goal:** Hallucinations, bias, privacy.
- **Guided Practice:** Good fit / bad fit for AI tasks.
- **Independent Work:** `ai-reflection-01.md`.
- **Homework:** AI Lens prompt #1.

### Lesson 3: Responsible AI for the Assistant
- **Goal:** Source-based answers; sample grounded prompt.
- **Guided Practice:** Class prompt with "I don't know" rule.
- **Independent Work:** Complete Responsible AI Checklist; push to GitHub.
- **Homework:** Prepare for Phase 3 (AI Math Bridge).
