# Phase 0: AI Literacy Foundation

## Phase Goal
Build a shared understanding of AI and generative AI before writing any application code, and connect that understanding directly to the AI School Assistant.

## Key Concepts
- What AI, machine learning, and generative AI mean.
- What AI can and cannot do (including hallucinations).
- Basic prompt engineering ideas.
- Responsible AI: accuracy, fairness, privacy, and human responsibility.

## Required Student Output
- Study notes for **AI for Everyone** and **Generative AI for Everyone**.
- Written responses to at least three AI Lens Discussion Prompts.
- A completed Responsible AI Checklist for the AI School Assistant idea.

## Suggested Class Activities
- Watch course modules together, then discuss key ideas in small groups.
- "Can AI do this?" sorting game: list tasks and decide if AI is a good fit.
- Spot-the-hallucination demo: show a confident but wrong AI answer.
- Draft a first one-sentence vision for each student's AI School Assistant.

## AI Lens Connection
Everything in this phase prepares students to build an **honest, source-based** assistant. Stress that the assistant must rely on school documents and say "I don't know" when unsure — concepts return in Phase 6 (RAG).
