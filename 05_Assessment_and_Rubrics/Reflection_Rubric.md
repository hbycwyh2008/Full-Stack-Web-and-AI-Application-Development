# Reflection Rubric

**Teacher-facing — simplified version may be exported to Student Hub**

---

## Criteria

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | Honest, specific reflection; connects challenges to learning; clear AI disclosure; concrete next steps |
| **Meets** | Addresses all prompts; describes what was built and learned; states AI use |
| **Approaching** | Vague or generic; missing AI disclosure; little evidence of self-analysis |
| **Not Yet** | Missing, copied without understanding, or does not match submitted work |

---

## Required Elements

- [ ] What was built or practiced
- [ ] What was learned (specific, not generic)
- [ ] Challenges and how they were addressed
- [ ] **AI use disclosure** (tools used, what was changed manually)
- [ ] Next steps or improvements

---

## Red Flags

- Reflection contradicts code quality or oral explanation
- Generic text that could apply to any assignment
- No mention of AI when code shows heavy AI-style patterns
- Identical reflections across students
