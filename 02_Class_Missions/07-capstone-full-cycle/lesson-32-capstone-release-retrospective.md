# Lesson 32 — Capstone Release, Demo, and Retrospective

## Mission
Release the product and explain the full chain of evidence from user problem to final iteration.

## Core Artifact
Public release + demo + lifecycle retrospective.

## Launch Challenge — 0–10
Students assemble eight evidence cards: research, requirement, design, issue, commit/PR, test, release, iteration. They must connect them into a causal story.

## Evidence / Resource Scan — 10–25
Final release checklist: deployed URL, primary task, responsive/accessibility checks, README, Figma link, issue/PR history, test evidence, AI product-decision memo, known limitations.

## Team Sensemaking — 25–40
Teams rehearse a demo that begins with **user problem and evidence**, not technology names.

## Studio Build — 40–65
Publish the release and final documentation. Prepare a short walkthrough showing one major decision that changed because of research or testing.

## Critique / Test — 65–80
Peer audience uses the product and challenges one design/engineering decision. Team answers with evidence or records the question as future work.

## Ship Evidence — 80–90
Write retrospective: what changed from first assumption → research → Figma → implementation → testing → release; what the team would maintain or redesign next.

## Success Evidence
Students can demonstrate the complete software-development lifecycle and explain trade-offs with evidence.
