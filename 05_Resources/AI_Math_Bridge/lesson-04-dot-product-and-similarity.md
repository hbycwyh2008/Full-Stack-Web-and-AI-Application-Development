# Lesson 4: Dot Product and Similarity

## Goal

Intuition for **dot product** as alignment or similarity — not heavy symbolic math.

## Key Ideas

- Dot product can measure how aligned two vectors are.
- Recommendation systems often compare vectors.
- AI systems compare numerical representations (embeddings, features).

## Activities

1. Simple 2D dot product with small numbers (calculator or NumPy preview).
2. Write `dot-product-notes.md` with one similarity example.
3. Discuss: two students with similar score vectors.
