# Talk Robin Rules

**Talk Robin** = structured pair discussion. Default: **pairs** (two students). Teacher assigns pairs; switch partners across lessons when helpful.

---

## Timing (4–5 Minutes Total)

| Step | Time | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Student A speaks | 45 sec | A explains their answer to B |
| Student B speaks | 45 sec | B explains their answer to A |
| Pair shared answer | 30 sec | Agree on one best answer or one question for teacher |
| Teacher cold call | 2–3 min | Teacher calls 2–3 students to share |

**Use a timer.** Stop when time ends — practice being concise.

---

## Two Rounds Per Lesson

### Talk Robin Round 1 — After Entry Check

**Purpose:** Check basic understanding and **reveal confusion** before the teacher explains the pattern.

**Your job:**

- Compare Entry Check answers with your partner
- Name one thing you are unsure about
- Do not copy your partner’s answer without understanding it

### Talk Robin Round 2 — Before Exit Evidence

**Purpose:** Summarize the **core pattern** and prepare to explain your **learning evidence**.

**Your job:**

- State the pattern in one sentence (e.g. “small change → commit → history”)
- Say what evidence you will submit today
- Be ready if the teacher calls on you

---

## How to Speak (45 Seconds)

1. **Claim** — “I think a commit is…”
2. **Evidence** — “In my repo, my last commit…”
3. **Question** — “I’m still unsure about…”

Avoid: reading the entire README aloud.

---

## Pair Rules

- Face each other or screen-share one repo — not phones off-task
- Both students speak every round
- If your partner is stuck, **hint** — do not take over their rebuild
- English or class language per teacher policy

---

## Teacher Cold Call

When called:

- Stand or unmute
- Answer in **your own words**
- It is OK to say: “I’m not sure, but I tried…” — then say what you tried

Oral explanation may include how you used AI and what **you** changed afterward.
