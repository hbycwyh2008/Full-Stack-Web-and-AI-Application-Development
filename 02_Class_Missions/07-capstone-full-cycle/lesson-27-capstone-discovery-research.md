# Lesson 27 — Capstone Discovery and User Research

## Mission
Choose a problem worth solving based on access to real users and evidence, not on which feature sounds impressive.

## Core Artifact
Capstone research plan + first research sessions.

## Launch Challenge — 0–10
Students pitch problems without proposing solutions. Peers mark which statements describe a user difficulty and which secretly contain a chosen feature.

## Evidence / Resource Scan — 10–25
Review the HCI research unit and previous research evidence. Identify a feasible user group the team can actually study/test.

## Team Sensemaking — 25–40
Teams compare candidate problems by user access, importance, scope, ethics/privacy, and ability to test within course time.

## Studio Build — 40–65
Select the problem, create the research plan, conduct or schedule research, and document assumptions that must be tested.

## Critique / Test — 65–80
Peer team challenges scope and evidence. Any problem statement based only on team preference returns to research.

## Ship Evidence — 80–90
Commit the plan/notes and open the capstone milestone.

## Success Evidence
The team can name the users, the observed problem, and the evidence still needed before design.
