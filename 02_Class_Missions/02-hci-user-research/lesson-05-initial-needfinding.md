# Lesson 05 — Initial Needfinding

**Length:** 45 minutes  
**Loop step:** Initial Needfinding  
**Mission:** Separate guesses from real unknowns, and choose how the team will learn from users before inventing features.  
**One Required Resource / Evidence Source:** Coursera video below + teacher needfinding scenario  
**Durable Evidence:** needfinding sheet (known / unknown / collection plan)

### Skill Warm-up video (required)

| Field | Assign this |
|---|---|

| Link | https://www.coursera.org/learn/design-research |

## 0–5 min — Skill Warm-up

Open the required video above. Watch only the assigned clip. Write two lines:

1. One thing a researcher did to learn about users.
2. One thing I am still unsure about.

If video access fails, read the teacher scenario and mark: **guess** vs **something we could check with users**.

## 5–9 min — Talk Robin 1

Pairs share:

- You: “What was done in the warm-up was…”
- Partner: “What was confusing was…”
- Together: “One thing we still do not know about our users is…”

## 9–14 min — Entry Check

Individually respond to this request:

> “Build something that helps students study better.”

List **three ideas** you might build. For each idea, label it **guess** or **need evidence**. Circle the biggest unknown.

## 14–19 min — Core Pattern

Teacher teaches the must-know idea:

**Needfinding** = finding what people struggle with and need, by collecting evidence (ask / watch / notes), **before** picking an app feature.

Show the three data moves on the board:

1. **Ask** — short questions about real past behavior  
2. **Observe** — watch someone do a task; write what you see  
3. **Notes** — keep quotes and actions separate from your opinions  

Groups start a **Needfinding Sheet**:

1. Project topic in one sentence  
2. Three things we already know  
3. Three things we do not know yet  
4. Which method we will try first (ask / observe / survey)

## 19–30 min — Guided Practice

Teams finish the Needfinding Sheet for their project (or the class study scenario):

- primary user (who we will learn from);
- situation where the problem happens;
- three unknowns written as questions to investigate;
- data we will **not** collect (names, grades, private chats, passwords);
- 30-second consent line they will use later (see printables).

Teacher checks that questions are about **behavior/context**, not “Would you use my app?”

## 30–40 min — Independent Rebuild

Individually rewrite this guess into a needfinding question:

> “Students need an AI study app.”

Write:

1. What is being guessed?  
2. What evidence would change your mind?  
3. One better question to ask a classmate.

## 40–45 min — Talk Robin 2 + Evidence

Submit Needfinding Sheet + individual rewrite. Pair share: one unknown and which collection method comes next.

## Success Evidence

Students can explain needfinding in their own words, mark guesses vs unknowns, and list a simple plan to collect user data (ask, observe, or survey).
