# AI for Everyone — Study Guide

**Course:** AI for Everyone  
**Instructor:** Andrew Ng  
**Provider:** DeepLearning.AI  
**Link:** https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/ai-for-everyone

Use this guide while you watch the course. Take notes in your own words. After each section, answer the **Connect to AI School Assistant** question.

---

## Before You Start

- Create a Notion page or document called **AI Literacy Notes**.
- You do not need to know coding for this course.
- Focus on understanding ideas you will use when building software later.

---

## Module 1: What Is AI?

### Key Ideas to Capture
- What is artificial intelligence in simple terms?
- What is machine learning?
- What is data, and why does AI need it?
- What is the difference between traditional software and AI?

### Connect to AI School Assistant
> If the AI School Assistant learns from school documents, what role does **data** play in your project?

---

## Module 2: Building AI Projects

### Key Ideas to Capture
- What is a typical AI project workflow?
- What roles exist on an AI team (even if one person does many jobs)?
- What is the difference between a demo and a real product?

### Connect to AI School Assistant
> Your final project is small, but it follows a real workflow: problem → data → model/API → interface → testing. Which step will be hardest for you?

---

## Module 3: AI in Your Organization / School

### Key Ideas to Capture
- What problems are worth solving with AI?
- What problems are **not** a good fit for AI?
- How do you spot a useful AI opportunity?

### Connect to AI School Assistant
> Why is "answer questions from school documents" a good AI use case? What would be a **bad** use case for this app?

---

## Module 4: What AI Can and Cannot Do

### Key Ideas to Capture
- What tasks is AI good at today?
- Where does AI often fail or disappoint?
- Why is "AI can do anything" a dangerous belief?

### Connect to AI School Assistant
> List three questions your assistant should answer well, and three questions it should **refuse** or handle carefully.

---

## Module 5: AI and Society

### Key Ideas to Capture
- How is AI changing work and learning?
- What are fairness and bias concerns?
- Why does responsible AI use matter in schools?

### Connect to AI School Assistant
> How could your assistant give unfair or incorrect advice? What rule would prevent that?

---

## Exit Reflection

Answer in 5–8 sentences:

1. What is AI, in your own words?
2. What surprised you most in this course?
3. How will these ideas help you build the AI School Assistant?

Save this reflection in your portfolio under **Learning Notes**.
