## Homework / Independent Study Principles

- Each week should have **one clear deliverable**, not many small tasks.
- Reading and watching videos happen mostly as homework; class time is for building and discussion.
- Every coding phase ends with an **AI usage reflection**.
- Encourage students to keep a weekly **Learning Journal** entry.

---

## Teacher Notes for Pacing Adjustment

> 根据学生实际进度灵活调整。Adjust flexibly based on real student progress.

- **If students fall behind on coding:** spend extra time on Phases 4–5; the AI literacy and portfolio phases can be lightly reviewed, not repeated.
- **If students are advanced:** add stretch goals (auth, multiple documents, better UI) in Phases 7–8.
- **Phase 0 is non-negotiable.** Do not skip AI literacy to save time — it underpins responsible final projects.
- **Phase 6 is the bridge.** If RAG concepts feel abstract, return to them during Phase 8 when building the real `/ask` feature.
- Build buffer days before each checkpoint for students who need to catch up.
