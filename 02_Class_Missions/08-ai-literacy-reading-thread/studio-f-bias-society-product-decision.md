# AI Studio F — Bias, Society, and the Product Decision

**Book spine:** assigned sections on bias, human consequences, and the broader limits/implications of AI.

## Activity
Teams analyze a proposed AI feature through four lenses: **who benefits, who can be harmed, what data/assumptions shape the output, what happens when it is wrong**.

## Discussion
The key question is not “Can we add AI?” but **“Does AI improve this user's problem enough to justify its errors, opacity, cost, privacy impact, and maintenance burden?”**

## Product Connection
Capstone teams make a written decision: **AI not needed / AI could be optional / AI is justified**. The decision must cite user research and technical/social limitations.

## Evidence
Commit an `ai-product-decision.md` memo. Students are rewarded for a well-supported decision, including deciding not to use AI.
