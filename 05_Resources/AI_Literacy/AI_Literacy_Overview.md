# AI Literacy Resources — Current Full Stack Route

**Core text:** Melanie Mitchell, *Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans*. [Author's page](https://melaniemitchell.me/aibook/).

Start with the [six-theme foundation path](../../02_Class_Missions/06-ai-literacy/foundation-path.md) and the [AI Literacy Checklist](../../04_Assessment/AI_Literacy_Checklist.md). Required reading happens in class; use short selected passages rather than full-chapter homework.

Themes: recognizing AI; learning/training/prediction; task input/output; failures and limits; purposeful generative-AI use and verification; privacy, disclosure and responsibility.

The [unit README](../../02_Class_Missions/06-ai-literacy/README.md) distinguishes foundation requirements from the preserved twelve-meeting reading extension. AI math, model training, API integration and an AI School Assistant product are not prerequisites. Students do not have to force AI into their final project.

## Existing Optional Materials

The older AI for Everyone and Generative AI for Everyone study guides, AI Lens prompts and Responsible AI checklist remain reference material. They are not two additional required courses or an obligation to build an AI School Assistant. Their older phase/product wording does not override the current foundation path.

The teacher may choose a relevant example as an explicit supplement; the selected Mitchell reading remains the conceptual spine. Follow the course [student AI policy](../../01_Student_Start/04_AI_Use_Policy_For_Students.md).
