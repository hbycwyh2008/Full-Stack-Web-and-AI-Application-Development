# AI Lens Discussion Prompts

Use these prompts in class discussions, small groups, or written reflections. Each prompt connects AI literacy to the **AI School Assistant** final project.

Answer in clear, complete sentences. There is no single "right" answer — but your answer should show **understanding**, not guessing.

---

## Section A: Understanding AI (from AI for Everyone)

### Prompt 1: What Is AI, Really?
Explain what AI is to a classmate who thinks AI is "just magic." Use one example from the AI School Assistant.

### Prompt 2: Good Problem vs. Bad Problem
Why is "help students find answers in the student handbook" a good AI problem? Give one school problem that AI should **not** solve alone.

### Prompt 3: Limits of AI
The AI School Assistant gives a confident but wrong answer about exam dates. What went wrong? What should the app do differently?

---

## Section B: Generative AI (from Generative AI for Everyone)

### Prompt 4: Why Documents Matter
Why can't we just ask an LLM "What is our school's late homework policy?" without providing the handbook text?

### Prompt 5: Prompt Design
Write a prompt your backend might use to answer: *"What are the rules for using AI in school?"* Include instructions to stay within the document and say "I don't know" if unsure.

### Prompt 6: Hallucinations
What is a hallucination? How would a user recognize one in the AI School Assistant? How would you reduce them?

---

## Section C: Responsible AI in School

### Prompt 7: Who Is Responsible?
A student blames the AI School Assistant for missing a deadline because the app gave bad advice. Who is responsible — the student, the developer, the school, or the AI? Explain your reasoning.

### Prompt 8: Fairness and Access
Could the AI School Assistant help some students more than others? What design choices would make it fairer?

### Prompt 9: Transparency
Should the app tell users they are talking to AI? Should it show its sources? Why or why not?

---

## Section D: From Literacy to Engineering

### Prompt 10: From Idea to Architecture
Draw or describe the path from a user's question to a final answer. Name each part: frontend, backend, documents, LLM.

### Prompt 11: Human + AI Workflow
When building the AI School Assistant with Cursor, what should **you** decide, and what might **AI** help with?

### Prompt 12: Final Project Vision
In 3–5 sentences, describe the AI School Assistant you want to build. Who is it for? What problem does it solve? What will you **not** let it do?

---

## Teacher Notes

> 讨论时要求学生引用课程中的概念，而不是空泛观点。Ask students to reference course concepts, not vague opinions. Use Prompts 4, 6, and 10 as strong bridges into Phase 6 (AI API + RAG Concepts).
