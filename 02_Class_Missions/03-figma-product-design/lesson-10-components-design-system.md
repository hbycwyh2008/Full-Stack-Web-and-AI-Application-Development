# Lesson 10 — Components, Design Systems, and Accessibility

**Mission:** Turn repeated interface decisions into a reusable, consistent system.

## Inconsistency Hunt (0–12)

Students inspect an intentionally inconsistent interface: near-identical buttons, random spacing, typography drift, missing states. They identify maintenance and usability problems.

## Design Tokens (12–28)

Create a small system for typography, spacing, radius, and semantic colors. The goal is consistency, not a huge style guide.

## Components + Variants (28–55)

Build reusable components for the product: buttons, inputs, cards/navigation as needed. Include meaningful variants and states such as default, hover/focus, disabled, error, selected.

Use Auto Layout where it supports responsive behavior.

## Accessibility Lab (55–70)

Check contrast, text sizing, focus visibility, target size, form labels, and color-only communication. Students identify at least two accessibility risks and revise them.

## Break the Component (70–82)

Swap with another team. Test components with longer text, shorter text, narrow frames, missing images, or error messages. Record failures.

## Revision + Ship (82–90)

Fix the highest-impact failures and publish a mini design-system page.

## Success Evidence

Students can explain why components reduce inconsistency and why accessibility must be designed before coding.
