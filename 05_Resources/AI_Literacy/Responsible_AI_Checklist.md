# Responsible AI Checklist

Complete this checklist **before** you start building the AI School Assistant. Use it again before your final demo.

This checklist turns AI literacy into **design decisions** for your project.

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

- [ ] I can explain **who** the AI School Assistant is for (students, teachers, parents).
- [ ] I can explain **what problem** it solves in one sentence.
- [ ] I have listed questions the app **should** answer.
- [ ] I have listed questions the app **should not** answer (or should refuse).

---

## 2. Data and Sources

- [ ] I know which **school documents** the assistant will use.
- [ ] I understand that answers must come from those documents, not the model's memory alone.
- [ ] I will **not** put private student data into the app without teacher approval.
- [ ] I know where document files will be stored and who can update them.

---

## 3. Accuracy and Honesty

- [ ] The app will say **"I don't know"** when it cannot find a reliable answer.
- [ ] The app will show **which document or section** an answer came from.
- [ ] I have a plan to test answers against the real handbook (not just "it looks right").
- [ ] I understand what a **hallucination** is and how to reduce it.

---

## 4. Fairness and Access

- [ ] The app is designed to help all intended users, not just one group.
- [ ] The language and UI are clear for high school students.
- [ ] I have considered what happens if a user asks in unclear or misleading ways.

---

## 5. Privacy and Security

- [ ] API keys and secrets will **not** be committed to GitHub.
- [ ] I know what user data (if any) the app stores.
- [ ] I follow school rules about AI and data use.

> _Placeholder: add school-specific privacy and AI policies here._

---

## 6. Transparency and AI Disclosure

- [ ] Users know they are interacting with an AI-powered tool.
- [ ] My README and portfolio explain how AI is used in the project.
- [ ] I can explain what **I** built vs. what **AI tools** helped generate.
- [ ] I completed an **AI Usage Reflection** for this project.

---

## 7. Human Responsibility

- [ ] I will test the app myself before submitting it.
- [ ] I will not submit code I cannot explain.
- [ ] I understand that **I** am responsible for the project, even if AI helped write parts of it.
- [ ] I will fix bugs and incorrect behavior — not ignore them.
