# Prompt Use Policy

**Private master curriculum — AI and Cursor prompt guidelines**

Designed by Morgan Wang

---

## Principles

1. **Prompts support learning** — they do not replace student thinking.
2. **Teacher prompts stay teacher-only** unless explicitly approved for students.
3. **Student prompts** must encourage explanation, debugging, and disclosure.
4. **Exported prompts** (Student Hub or Classroom) must be reviewed for IP and safety.

---

## Classification

| Type | May share with students? | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Teacher demo / walkthrough prompts | No | `Teacher_Prompts/` |
| Student reflection / learning prompts | Yes (when reviewed) | `Student_Allowed_Prompts/` |
| Grading / rubric feedback prompts | No | `AI_Feedback_Prompts/` |
| Curriculum maintenance prompts | No | `Cursor_Refactor_Prompts/` |

---

## Before Exporting a Prompt to Students

- [ ] Remove internal planning comments
- [ ] Remove answer keys or solution hints beyond appropriate scaffolding
- [ ] Require AI use disclosure in the expected output
- [ ] Align with [AI_Use_Policy_Student_Version.md](../01_Student_Hub_Export/AI_Use_Policy_Student_Version.md)
