# Phase 0: AI Literacy Foundation

## Phase Goal
Students build a shared understanding of AI and generative AI before writing application code. This foundation connects directly to the **AI School Assistant** final project.

## Lesson Objectives
- Explain what AI and generative AI are in plain language.
- Describe what AI can and cannot do.
- Use basic prompt engineering ideas.
- Complete a Responsible AI Checklist for the final project.

## Required Tools
- DeepLearning.AI account (or Coursera access)
- Notion or document for study notes

## Required Resources
- `00_AI_Literacy/AI_Literacy_Overview.md`
- `00_AI_Literacy/AI_for_Everyone_Study_Guide.md`
- `00_AI_Literacy/Generative_AI_for_Everyone_Study_Guide.md`
- **AI for Everyone** — https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/ai-for-everyone
- **Generative AI for Everyone** — https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/generative-ai-for-everyone

## Student Tasks
- Complete both DeepLearning.AI courses using the study guides.
- Answer at least three prompts from `00_AI_Literacy/AI_Lens_Discussion_Prompts.md`.
- Complete `00_AI_Literacy/Responsible_AI_Checklist.md` for the AI School Assistant.
- Save study notes and reflections in portfolio **Learning Notes**.

## Teacher Notes
> AI 素养不是独立理论课，而是最终项目的地基。AI literacy is not a separate theory class — it is the foundation for the final project. Use discussion prompts 4, 6, and 10 to bridge into Phase 6 (RAG concepts).

## Exit Ticket
- In one sentence: what is generative AI?
- Name one thing the AI School Assistant must **not** do.

## Submission Requirement
- Portfolio learning notes + three discussion responses + completed Responsible AI Checklist.
