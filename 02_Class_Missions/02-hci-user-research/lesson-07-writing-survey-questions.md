# Lesson 07 — Survey, Bias Check, and Evidence → Need

**Length:** 45 minutes  
**Loop step:** Survey → synthesize → define need  
**CS6750 backbone:** **3.3** Needfinding and Requirements Gathering  
**Quality control:** wording bias · sampling / voluntary-response bias · overgeneralization  
**Mission:** Write a short survey that measures useful patterns, then combine survey/interview/observation evidence into 2–3 verifiable user needs.  
**Durable Evidence:** 5-question survey + sample check + evidence synthesis + 2–3 micro-requirements

## 0–5 min — Skill Warm-up

Write:

1. one rule for a good survey question;
2. one reason a survey of “whoever answers” may not represent all users.

## 5–9 min — Talk Robin 1

Fix:

- “Would you use our awesome app?”
- “Do you like reminders and dark mode?”
- “How much will AI improve your grades?”

Name the problem in each question.

## 9–14 min — Entry Check

A team surveys only students in its own friend group.

Answer:

- What can the team still learn?
- What should the team **not** claim?
- Who might be missing?

## 14–20 min — Core Pattern

### Question rules

1. Prefer **past/current behavior** over hypothetical future promises.
2. Ask **one thing** per question.
3. Do not praise, sell, or push an answer in the wording.
4. Use open questions for stories; ratings/options for patterns.
5. Avoid unnecessary private data.
6. Pilot-test the question before sending.

### Sample check

```text
Who answered?
→ Who did not?
→ Could those groups behave differently?
→ How strong can our claim actually be?
```

A small convenience sample can still generate useful clues. It cannot automatically represent every user.

### Evidence synthesis

```text
quote + action + survey pattern
→ possible need
→ check for contradictory evidence
→ verifiable need statement
```

Micro-requirement form:

> **When [situation], the user can [task/outcome] so that [success].**

## 20–31 min — Guided Practice

Teams draft a **5-question survey** based on Lessons 05–06:

- at least 2 open questions;
- at least 1 frequency/rating question;
- zero “Would you use our app?” questions;
- consent line;
- sample plan: who will answer + who may be missing.

Peer-test the survey. Rewrite at least one weak item.

Then complete the Evidence Synthesis table:

| Evidence | Source | Supports what need? | Contradiction / uncertainty? |
|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |

## 31–40 min — Independent Rebuild

Write **2–3 need / micro-requirement statements**.

For each, add:

- evidence supporting it;
- one bias / limitation;
- what additional evidence would increase confidence.

## 40–45 min — Talk Robin 2 + Evidence

Submit survey + sample check + evidence table + micro-requirements.

Exit sentence:

> “We have evidence for ___, but we are still uncertain about ___ because ___.”

## Success Evidence

Students can write non-leading survey questions, recognize a weak sample, avoid overgeneralizing, and turn multiple evidence sources into testable user-need statements.
