# AI Literacy — Grade 7 Canonical 45-Minute Classroom Flow

This is the Grade 7 adaptation of the course's required [Classroom Flow](../shared/classroom-flow.md). The **names, order, and purpose of the seven blocks remain unchanged**. Every lesson file uses these exact times.

## Seven Required Blocks

| Time | Block | Student role | Teacher role |
|---:|---|---|---|
| **0–3 min** | **Entry Point Check** | Make one individual prediction, classification, or judgement before reading | Reveal prior thinking; do not explain Mitchell's answer yet |
| **3–17 min** | **Individual Learning** | Read 300–450 assigned words in one or two chunks; mark C (claim), E (evidence/example), and ? (unclear); pause to paraphrase each chunk | Point to the exact start and stop locations; pronounce/gloss no more than four required words; optionally read the first paragraph aloud from the same book |
| **17–22 min** | **Talk Round 1** | Use a sentence frame to compare one claim and one piece of book evidence with a partner | Listen for one common reading barrier and one productive disagreement |
| **22–28 min** | **Entry Points Check / Teacher Diagnosis** | Show one group claim, evidence detail, and uncertainty | Diagnose from student work; clarify only the demonstrated barrier, then stop |
| **28–38 min** | **Guided Practice / Mission Task** | Complete one small organizer using the assigned book case | Model the first row or link, then coach evidence use and claim boundaries |
| **38–43 min** | **Exit Check** | Independently complete the four-line Learning Log | Check individual claim, evidence, boundary, and revised thought; do not add a second exit task |
| **43–45 min** | **Submission** | Submit the named organizer and Learning Log entry | Verify both items are present and the evidence is attributable to the assigned passage |

Total: **45 minutes**.

## Individual Learning Routine

The teacher writes the exact chapter, content anchor, and page range where everyone can see it. Then students use this routine:

1. **Chunk 1:** read, mark C / E / ?, and write a five-to-ten-word margin paraphrase.
2. **Pause:** compare the paraphrase with the passage; reread one difficult sentence if needed.
3. **Chunk 2, when assigned:** repeat the same process.
4. **Ready line:** complete “Mitchell's main point here is ___ because ___.”

Students who finish early reread for a second evidence detail or improve the ready line. They do not move to an outside source.

## One Required Resource Rule

The sole content input is Melanie Mitchell's *Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans*.

Allowed access supports:

- an exact page/location cue;
- a plain-language gloss for no more than four words;
- the C / E / ? marking code;
- sentence frames and blank organizers;
- teacher oral reading of the opening paragraph from the assigned selection;
- a teacher-created hypothetical used only after reading to test transfer.

Not used as required input:

- videos or podcasts;
- news, product, or research-summary articles;
- live AI/chatbot demonstrations;
- online chapter summaries;
- a second AI textbook or course;
- AI-generated summaries in place of reading.

## Grade 7 Reading Volume

- Default: **300–450 English words total** per meeting.
- Structure: one continuous selection or two chunks of no more than about 225 words each.
- Time: **14 minutes**, including marking and a short paraphrase.
- Maximum: do not exceed 450 words unless class evidence across several meetings shows that students are ready.
- Page count is only an estimate; word count and conceptual density control the selection.

Use the [Required In-Class Reading Map](required-reading-map.md). Do not solve a timing problem by assigning the remainder at home.

## Teacher Diagnosis Limit

Teacher Diagnosis is not a planned lecture. Use this sequence:

1. display one student/group claim;
2. point to the exact sentence or concept causing difficulty;
3. clarify or reread only that part;
4. check with one new student paraphrase;
5. begin the Mission Task.

## Evidence and Finish-Up Rule

Each meeting ends with:

- one small in-class reasoning artifact; and
- one four-line Learning Log entry, which is the Exit Check.

There is no separate routine homework. If the log is unfinished, only the remaining lines may be completed later, normally within five minutes.
