## Mission Goal

Today you will learn **how this course works before you begin coding**.

By the end of class, you should be able to explain:

- what you will eventually build in this course;
- how a normal class moves from learning to practice to rebuilding and sharing;
- why working code once is not the same as mastery;
- how AI can support learning without replacing your thinking;
- what is expected from your grade band.

## Part 5 — Your Grade-Band Expectation

Both Middle School and High School students use the same major tools and course pathway. The difference is the expected level of independence and mastery.

### Middle School Track

**Build · Modify · Explain**

You will receive more scaffolding, starter code, checklists, and guided debugging. You should be able to build with support, make meaningful changes, and explain what you did.

### High School Track

**Rebuild · Debug · Integrate · Transfer**

You will receive progressively less scaffolding. You should increasingly rebuild patterns, debug independently, connect systems, and use your skills in new situations.
