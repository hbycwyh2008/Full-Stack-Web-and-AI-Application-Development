# Figma Design Cycle — Unit Test

**Covers:** Lessons 09–11  
**Time:** 35 minutes  
**Total:** 30 points  
**Mode:** Individual · No AI · Figma allowed only for Part E  
**Purpose:** Check whether you understand and can apply **Prototype → Evaluate → Iterate**.

---

## What this test measures

You should be able to:

1. explain why prototypes exist;
2. choose an appropriate prototype fidelity;
3. identify affordance/signifier, mapping, and constraint problems;
4. write a usable no-coaching task;
5. distinguish evaluation evidence from taste/assumption;
6. prioritize revisions from evidence;
7. revise and retest rather than treating the first prototype as final.

# Part C — Evaluation (8 points)

### 7. — 2 pts

Rewrite this bad usability-test instruction:

> “Click the + button, choose Science, type the homework, and press Save.”

as a **goal-based task**.

### 8. — 2 pts

Why should the facilitator avoid coaching while the participant is doing the task?

### 9. — 2 pts

Classify each statement as **E = evidence**, **T = taste**, or **A = assumption**.

- ___ “The tester tapped the wrong icon twice.”
- ___ “The interface looks boring.”
- ___ “The tester said, ‘Where do I save?’”
- ___ “Students will obviously understand the calendar.”

### 10. — 2 pts

During a test, the participant pauses for 8 seconds, opens the wrong screen, goes back, and asks, “Where is the assignment?”

Write one **MUST or SHOULD revision** and cite the evidence that justifies it.

Required:

- Screen 1 has a clear task title/input and primary action.
- Screen 2 clearly shows success.
- Primary action is clickable from Screen 1 → Screen 2.
- The main action has a clear signifier.
- Use simple low-fi styling; do not spend time on decoration.
