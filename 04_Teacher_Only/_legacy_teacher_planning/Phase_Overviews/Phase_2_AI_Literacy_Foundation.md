This course is **AI application engineering** built on **AI literacy**. You will build an app that answers from documents — you must understand risks, limits, and responsible use **before** you wire APIs and RAG in later phases.

## In-Class Learning Flow

### 1. Skill Warm-up

**You do:** Finish sentence — “I think AI is ___.” Read one short module intro or watch assigned clip.

**Produce:** One paragraph draft for `ai-literacy/README.md`.

### 2. Talk Robin Round 1

- **You:** “One thing AI is good at in school is…”
- **Partner:** “One thing AI should NOT be trusted to do alone is…”
- **Together:** One confusion to clear in Core Pattern block.

### 3. Entry Check

1. Define AI vs generative AI in one sentence each.
2. What is a hallucination?
3. Where will your Phase 2 evidence live on GitHub?

### 4. Core Pattern

```text
learn concept → take notes → reflect in own words → commit to ai-literacy/ → connect to AI School Assistant
```

### 5. Guided Practice

**You complete with class:**

1. Start `ai-literacy/README.md` — course name, your name, learning goals for literacy
2. Notes from **AI for Everyone** (selected modules — teacher assigns)
3. `ai-reflection-01.md` — limits and risks
4. Start **Responsible AI Checklist** from `05_Resources/AI_Literacy/`

### 6. Independent Rebuild

**Checklist:**

- [ ] `ai-literacy/README.md` with real content
- [ ] At least one reflection file in your own words
- [ ] Notes on hallucinations, bias, or privacy
- [ ] One **AI Lens** response (prompts in `05_Resources/AI_Literacy/AI_Lens_Discussion_Prompts.md`)
- [ ] Meaningful commits on GitHub
- [ ] You can explain one responsible AI rule for the final project

**AI rule:** Hints OK; do not paste full reflections from AI without editing and understanding.

### 7. Talk Robin Round 2 + Evidence

- Share one rule you would enforce in the AI School Assistant
- Confirm Exit Evidence list
- Submit before leaving
