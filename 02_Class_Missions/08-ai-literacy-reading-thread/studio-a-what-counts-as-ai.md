# AI Studio A — What Counts as AI?

**Book spine:** assigned opening sections of Melanie Mitchell, _Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans_.

## Activity
Before reading, teams sort familiar systems into **AI / not AI / depends on definition** and write the rule they used. After reading, each student contributes one passage-based idea in their own words, then the team revises its definition.

## Product Connection
Choose one feature people often call “smart.” Separate the **observable capability** from claims such as “understands,” “thinks,” or “knows.”

## Evidence
Commit a claim–evidence–limitation note plus one definition the student changed and why.
